xMediaTool Privacy Policy for Opera

xMediaTool collects, organizes, and saves posts and media you can view on X in Opera.

Local library and exports

On supported X and Twitter pages, the extension reads post details from the page and media responses X already sent to your browser. When you capture content, xMediaTool stores post text, media and post links, author names and handles, post timestamps, engagement counts, and source X page URLs in its local library. Opera also stores your collections, preferences, favorites, and xMediaTool download records on your device.

The extension reads JSON library backups you choose to import. Downloads and exports write the selected content to your device. You can copy an individual media link to your clipboard.

Requests to X

When you start a batch, Opera opens the X or Twitter URLs you entered. Previews and downloads request media directly from pbs.twimg.com and video.twimg.com. Opera can send applicable cookies with its requests to X. X handles those requests under its privacy practices. X Privacy Policy

The extension uses captured content and settings on your device. The publisher receives no library records, support form data, or analytics from the Opera extension.

Storage and control

You can remove records from the Library and Posts tabs. The browser keeps preferences and download records until cleared. Use Opera’s download manager and your operating system to control saved files and the browser’s own history. Removing the extension clears its local library and settings.

Opera may hold a temporary copy of media while xMediaTool checks the file and saves it. The extension releases its staged data when the download finishes or is canceled.

Support

Use the Reported issues section of the xMediaTool listing in Opera Add-ons. For a privacy question or a bug, describe the issue using the extension’s version and the steps that reproduce it. Opera handles feedback submitted through its site.
