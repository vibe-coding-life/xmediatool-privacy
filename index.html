xMediaTool Privacy Policy

xMediaTool helps you collect, organize, and save media and posts from X in your browser.

Information processed in your browser

When an X page loads, xMediaTool reads media details from the page and from post responses X has already delivered to your browser. When you capture posts, your local library can store the post text and links, author names and handles, timestamps, engagement counts, media URLs and thumbnails, accessibility text, and the X page where the item appeared. Your browser also stores the tags, collections, favorites, settings, and download records you use in the extension.

When you import a JSON library backup, xMediaTool reads the file you select and adds supported media records to your local library. When you export items, xMediaTool writes the selected records or files to your device. Exports can contain the post text, links, media, and related metadata you selected. Copying a media link puts the selected URL on your clipboard.

Network requests

When you preview or download media, xMediaTool requests it directly from X’s media servers at pbs.twimg.com and video.twimg.com. When you start a batch, the browser opens the X or Twitter pages you entered. X receives the information associated with those requests under its own privacy practices. Your browser may send standard request information and any applicable cookies from your existing X session. xMediaTool does not read or store your X login credentials or send them to its publisher.

xMediaTool does not automatically send your library, post contents, or download history to the publisher. It does not send analytics or advertising data, sell user data, or share data with brokers. The publisher has no automatic access to your extension storage or downloaded files. X Privacy Policy

Support messages and problem reports

The support buttons open a form in the extension and fetch a math question from devnull.casa. When you send a message or report, the extension sends the form type, subject, your description, optional reply email, and math challenge response to the publisher’s support endpoint. Firefox asks for permission to share your support message, any reply email you enter, and any diagnostics you attach.

xMediaTool keeps a short log of extension actions, outcomes, error categories, counts, and times in browser session storage. The log clears when the browser session ends. Preview session log shows that activity, the browser’s major version, the extension version, and the current sidebar tab. Include session diagnostics starts selected, and Send report with log sends that snapshot with the report. Clear the checkbox to send the report by itself.

The publisher hosts the support service on devnull.casa. Cloudflare proxies HTTPS traffic to that server. The service stores accepted tickets with their time and reference number and emails them to the publisher’s support inbox. The support operator reads the submitted information to investigate and respond to your request. A confirmation means the server stored the ticket. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

The support endpoint receives the requesting device’s IP address through its HTTPS proxy. The service stores a keyed hash of that address to count submission attempts and enforce a temporary block after repeated requests. Rate-limit records expire and are removed by later submissions. Support messages remain on the server and in the publisher’s inbox until removed.

Storage and control

The library, preferences, and extension download records stay in local browser storage until removed or until the browser clears the extension’s data. You can remove captured media and posts from their library views. Removing a library record leaves any exported or downloaded file on your device. For a full reset, remove the extension and clear any remaining extension data through your browser. Use the browser download manager or your operating system to remove download history and saved files where applicable.

The extension can hold temporary media data on your device while it verifies and saves a file. It clears the staged data as the download finishes or is canceled. The browser may retain ordinary network cache and download records according to its own settings.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use disclosure

xMediaTool uses and transfers information received from Chrome APIs in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension uses the data to provide its capture, organization, and download functions. Chrome Web Store User Data Policy
